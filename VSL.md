# MacroSnap VSL — voice-over script (~64 s)

The landing page plays a motion-design VSL built in code (`components/landing/Vsl.tsx`), 8 scenes, muted.
To replace it with a real video, record this script over a screen capture of the motion VSL and save it as `public/vsl.mp4`: the page switches to the video automatically on the next deploy.

| # | Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:07 | A timer running up: "time spent logging one lunch" | "Logging one lunch shouldn't take ten minutes. But it does." |
| 2 | 0:07–0:15 | Typing "chicken bowl", a list from 88 to 1,240 calories | "You search. You scroll through forty versions of the same meal. Then you guess." |
| 3 | 0:15–0:22 | A two-week calendar, the streak breaks on day 8 | "So by week two, most people just stop logging. It's not willpower. It's friction." |
| 4 | 0:22–0:29 | A camera shutter | "So what if you just… took a photo?" |
| 5 | 0:29–0:38 | Scan line over a chicken bowl, food labels pop in | "MacroSnap reads every food on your plate: the rice, the chicken, the avocado, even the beans." |
| 6 | 0:38–0:47 | Calories count up, protein / carbs / fat fill in | "And in seconds, you've got your calories, protein, carbs and fat. No typing. No weighing." |
| 7 | 0:47–0:56 | A streak counting up to 30 days | "Every meal logged. Every day. That's how you actually hit your goals." |
| 8 | 0:56–1:04 | The camera button, glowing | "Snap your first meal right now. It's free to try." |

Tone: friendly, quick, US English, like telling a friend about a trick you found. No claims about weight loss.
